📊 How It Works

This system uses an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) trained on the UCI Banknote Authentication Dataset to classify banknotes as genuine or forged based on wavelet-transformed image features.

  • Variance: Variance of the wavelet-transformed image
  • Skewness: Skewness of the wavelet-transformed image
  • Curtosis: Curtosis of the wavelet-transformed image
  • Entropy: Entropy of the image
